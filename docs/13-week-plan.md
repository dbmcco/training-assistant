# 13-Week Half-Marathon Training Plan — Braydon McCormick
Window: Mon Aug 3 2026 -> Sun Nov 1 2026.  A-race: Hartford Half (Oct 10).  Tune-up: Surftown Half (Sep 13).

ATHLETE BASELINE (use these real numbers):
- Trained endurance athlete. Finished IRONMAN 70.3 Western Mass June 7 2026 (56mi bike + 13.1mi run @ HR 154). Not a beginner.
- Half-marathon race pace ~9:42-10:05/mi (Surftown 2025 9:42/mi HR162; Stonington Half May 2026 10:04/mi HR166).
- Easy/long aerobic 9:50-10:45/mi @ HR 150-160. Tempo/threshold 8:50-9:20/mi @ HR 168-174. 5K ~8:00-8:20/mi.
- RHR 48-53, max HR ~192. HR runs higher in heat — govern by effort on hot days.
- Longest run in last 6 weeks is 5.8mi; aerobic base intact but long-run volume must ramp per the targets.

STRENGTH (mandatory, concrete, all 3 pillars): CORE (dead bug, bird dog, Pallof press, side plank, plank, hollow hold), FUNCTIONAL lower-body (goblet squat, RDL, reverse lunge, step-up, glute bridge, single-leg RDL), UPPER BODY (push-up, DB row, overhead press, renegade row). Progress reps/load week to week. Every strength day lists exercises with sets x reps.

RACES: Surftown Half Sun Sep 13 (W6, B-race tune-up). Hartford Half Sat Oct 10 (W10, A-race GOAL).
